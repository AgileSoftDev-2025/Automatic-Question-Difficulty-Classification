Help Center & FAQ

Have questions? We have answers. Find everything you need to know about Bloomers right here.

Bloomers is an intelligent web application that helps educators and content creators automatically analyze and classify questions based on Bloom's Taxonomy (C1-C6). It ensures cognitive diversity in assessment materials, making it easier to create well-balanced evaluations that test different levels of thinking.

Currently, Bloomers supports PDF files only. We recommend uploading clear, text-based PDFs for the best classification results. Make sure your questions are formatted properly and easy to read.

You can upload PDF files up to 10MB in size. This should be more than enough for most question sets and assessments.

There are a few common reasons why an upload might fail:

  • Wrong format: Only PDF files are accepted
  • File too large: Your file exceeds the 10MB limit
  • Empty file: The PDF contains no readable content
  • No file selected: You need to choose a file before clicking "Classify"

Make sure your PDF is properly formatted and contains clear, readable questions.

You can try Bloomers without an account, but creating one gives you significant benefits:

  • Guest Users: Your classification is processed but results are temporary, stored for only 1 hour in our cache
  • Registered Users: Your files and results are saved permanently to your account, giving you access to your complete classification history, status tracking, and the ability to re-download files anytime

We recommend creating a free account to get the most out of Bloomers!

When you see a "Pending" status, it means your file has been successfully received and is waiting in the queue to be processed by our AI classification system. Processing is usually quick but may take a few moments depending on file size and server load.

Simply refresh the page after a short time to see your updated status (Completed or Failed). You'll receive your full classification results once processing is complete.

  • Download: This retrieves your original uploaded PDF file, allowing you to save a copy back to your device
  • Delete: This permanently removes the file and its classification results from your history. This action cannot be undone, so use it carefully

Note: The Download button gives you the original file, not a report of the classification results.

Bloom's Taxonomy classifies questions by cognitive complexity:

  • C1 (Remember): Recall facts and basic concepts
  • C2 (Understand): Explain ideas or concepts
  • C3 (Apply): Use information in new situations
  • C4 (Analyze): Draw connections and distinguish between parts
  • C5 (Evaluate): Justify decisions and make judgments
  • C6 (Create): Produce new or original work

Bloomers automatically identifies which level each question belongs to, helping you balance your assessments.
